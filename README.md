# Sean McConoughey | Aerospace, Propulsion, and Test Engineer

Sean McConoughey is an aerospace engineering student, test engineer, propulsion developer, and aerospace cybersecurity researcher based at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University. My work spans liquid rocket propulsion, launch and ground operations, test automation, instrumentation and data acquisition, embedded systems, autonomous-system resilience, and applied AI/ML.

I am most useful where hardware, software, and operations meet: defining a test, building the fixture and controls, integrating sensors and actuators, running the system, interpreting the data, and fixing what fails in the real world.

## Rocket propulsion and launch operations

### Experimental Rocket Propulsion Lab (ERPL) — President
**Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University · 2025–2026**

ERPL is a multidisciplinary student organization that designs, manufactures, instruments, and hot-fires liquid rocket engines and develops complete flight vehicles. As president, I led technical programs across engines, fluids, electronics, flight systems, safety, operations, fundraising, and external partnerships.

My work included:

- Leading Project MOE, a student-developed LOX/isopropyl-alcohol bipropellant rocket designed around a roughly 2,250 lbf engine and a 60,000 ft, Mach 2+ mission.
- Coordinating design reviews, test readiness, operational procedures, safety planning, budgets, sponsors, and test-site logistics.
- Supporting regeneratively cooled and ablative LOX/RP-1 engine development.
- Directing ground systems, avionics, propulsion, structures, and flight integration across a large student engineering team.
- Building partnerships with aerospace companies, manufacturers, university leadership, and research organizations.

### Project Triton — Electronics Lead and Ground Controller
**2024–2025**

- Led avionics, instrumentation, telemetry, and ground-control development for ERPL’s approximately 1,000 lbf ethanol/LOX engine.
- Integrated pressure transducers, load cells, thermocouples, solenoid-valve control, and Teensy-based data acquisition.
- Developed the engine-monitoring interface, abort logic, sensor validation, and test procedures.
- Served as ground controller during hot-fire testing at Cecil Spaceport.
- Helped deliver the most powerful liquid rocket engine test in ERPL history at the time.

### PANDA distributed avionics and DAQ

Designed and developed a distributed rocket engine and vehicle control architecture with:

- 16 pressure-transducer channels
- 6 load-cell channels
- 6 thermocouple channels
- 12 solenoid-control channels
- High-rate data acquisition and real-time telemetry
- Distributed communications, signal conditioning, fault handling, and ground-control integration

The system was designed for liquid-engine testing and high-altitude flight operations, with an emphasis on serviceability, redundancy, electromagnetic compatibility, and rapid field troubleshooting.

### Project Saguaro and regenerative engine development

Contributed combustion-chamber, thermal, flow, cycle, and manufacturability work to LOX/kerosene engine concepts. Work has included injector and chamber development, regenerative cooling, ablative systems, pressure-fed fluid architecture, CAD, FEA, hand calculations, design reviews, and test planning.
